# Code of conduct

RepoSentinel aims to be a welcoming project for people of every background
and experience level.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful and patient, especially with people who are new to security
  or to open source.
* Discuss ideas and code, not people. Give and accept constructive feedback.
* Assume good faith, and ask before assuming.
* Never share real credentials, personal data or other people's private
  information in issues, pull requests or examples.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, insults, discriminatory language, personal attacks, sexual
content, threats, publishing someone's private information, and sustained
disruption of discussions.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and
may block participants who do not follow this code.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [@ItzMeVishwas](https://github.com/ItzMeVishwas)
on GitHub. For content that should not stay public (for example harassment or
someone's private information), you can also use GitHub's **Report content**
option on the comment, issue or pull request; GitHub reviews those reports.
Security vulnerabilities are reported as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
